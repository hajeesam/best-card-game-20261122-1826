EMBERFALL DUEL

How to play

You duel Cinder, keeper of the last ember. Both of you start at 30 life.

  • Each turn you gain embers equal to the turn number (up to 10). Embers refill every turn.
  • Play a card by clicking it, or press 1–7 to pick a card and Enter to play it.
  • Press Space or E, or the End Turn button, to pass to Cinder.
  • Resonance: play two cards of the same suit in one turn and the second one grows stronger (+2 damage, heal or shield, +1 draw).
  • Shield soaks damage first, then fades when your next turn begins — time your guard.
  • Reduce Cinder to 0 life to win. If your life reaches 0, the ember goes out.

Flame burns · Tide draws · Leaf heals · Stone shields.
